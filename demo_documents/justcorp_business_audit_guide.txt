JustCorp Labs Business Audit Guide

Purpose of the Business Audit

The JustCorp Labs business audit helps identify where a business can improve its digital presence, customer journey, data visibility, and operational efficiency.

The audit is designed to find practical opportunities for growth.

Audit Areas

1. Website and Online Presence
The audit checks whether the business has a professional website, whether the website is easy to understand, whether services are clearly explained, and whether customers know how to contact the business.

2. Customer Journey
The audit reviews how a customer discovers the business, understands the offer, asks questions, and decides to buy or book a service.

3. Lead Capture
The audit checks whether the business has a way to collect customer leads, contact details, inquiries, or booking requests.

4. Follow-Up Process
The audit reviews whether the business follows up with potential customers consistently. Poor follow-up can lead to lost sales.

5. Data and Reporting
The audit checks whether the business tracks important metrics such as sales, customers, profit, stock, inquiries, and marketing performance.

6. Automation Opportunities
The audit identifies repetitive tasks that could be automated. Examples include follow-up messages, email replies, report generation, data entry, and reminders.

7. AI Opportunities
The audit checks whether the business could benefit from AI tools such as a knowledge base chatbot, customer support assistant, AI sales agent, or document assistant.

Audit Output

After the audit, JustCorp Labs can provide a short recommendation report. The report may include website improvements, dashboard ideas, automation opportunities, AI use cases, and suggested next steps.

Goal of the Audit

The goal is not just to find problems. The goal is to show the business how better systems can help attract customers, save time, improve decisions, and support growth.
