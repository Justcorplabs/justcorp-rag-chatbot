JustCorp Labs Client Onboarding Guide

Overview

The JustCorp Labs onboarding process is designed to understand the client’s business clearly before building a solution.

Step 1: Discovery

JustCorp Labs starts by learning about the client’s business, goals, customers, current systems, and main challenges.

Step 2: Problem Definition

The team defines the main problem to solve. This could be a weak website, poor customer follow-up, lack of data visibility, repetitive manual work, or the need for an AI assistant.

Step 3: Solution Proposal

JustCorp Labs proposes a practical solution. The solution may be a website, dashboard, automation workflow, AI chatbot, AI sales agent, or data analytics project.

Step 4: Build Plan

The project is broken into clear steps. The client understands what will be built, what tools will be used, and what the expected outcome is.

Step 5: Development

JustCorp Labs builds the system. The first version focuses on solving the core business problem without unnecessary complexity.

Step 6: Testing

The system is tested to make sure it works correctly. For AI systems, testing includes checking whether the assistant gives useful answers and avoids unsupported claims.

Step 7: Feedback

The client reviews the system and gives feedback. JustCorp Labs uses this feedback to improve the product.

Step 8: Delivery

The final version is delivered with instructions, documentation, and guidance on how to use it.

Step 9: Future Improvements

After delivery, JustCorp Labs can suggest improvements such as automation upgrades, analytics dashboards, AI features, or website enhancements.

Client Responsibilities

Clients should provide clear information, business documents, branding materials, access to relevant tools, and feedback during development.

Project Goal

The goal is to build a practical system that helps the client operate better, attract customers, save time, and grow.
